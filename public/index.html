Privacy & limitations (please read)
  • Document reading, preview, editing and PDF creation run in your browser. For extraction, the case text is sent through this site’s server to Groq’s AI API; if the AI service is unavailable, basic rule-based extraction is used. No patient data is stored by this page; closing the tab clears the case. Use anonymised data unless your own policy permits otherwise.
  • Extraction uses Claude and can still misplace or miss items: always review the AI Extraction screen against the source. Unplaceable text is listed under Patient Profile.
  • Bengali handout: fixed wording is Bengali; clinician-written content is carried over in English for you to translate in Edit mode. There is no machine-translation engine in this build.
  • Legacy .doc cannot be read; save as .docx. Scanned (image-only) PDFs are not supported (no OCR).
  • PDFs are page images of the preview with the letterhead repeated on every page; text is not selectable, and a page break may fall through a line of text.
